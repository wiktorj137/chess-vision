# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project is aimed at beginners learning to see a chessboard. That sets the
tone: we are patient with people who are learning, whether they are learning
chess or learning to code.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being welcoming to people whose first pull request this is
- accepting that "this position looks wrong to me" is a valid bug report even
  without code attached
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- focusing on what is best for people using the extension

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- rating-based condescension of any kind
- harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- publishing others' private information without permission
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with
this Code of Conduct, and may ban temporarily or permanently any contributor
for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Report problems by opening an issue or contacting a maintainer privately. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
